## What it is

**margin-watch** keeps an eye on the loan-to-value ratio of a Bitcoin-backed loan so you don't have to. It tracks price against your loan terms and alerts you well before you get near a margin call or liquidation.

## Why I built it

It's the natural companion to [Bitcoin, Borrow, Die](/projects/bitcoin-borrow-die). The strategy only works if you never get forced to sell, and the failure mode is a fast drawdown while you're not watching. Newsletter readers kept asking how they should monitor their positions.

## How it’s built

- A focused SaaS product built with **AI coding agents**, from data model to billing.
- Price monitoring against user-defined loan terms, with threshold alerts.

## What it proves

That the newsletter audience is a product-discovery channel: I found the problem in reader replies, then shipped the tool.
