## What it is

**BitStack** is portfolio and wealth software for Bitcoiners. Its position is simple: buy Bitcoin, borrow against it, never sell. The app helps you live that way safely.

- **Multi-asset tracking** across self-custody wallets, Bitcoin ETFs, MicroStrategy (MSTR) and stocks.
- **Bitcoin-backed loan monitoring** with liquidation-price alerts, so a drawdown never catches you off guard.
- **Forecasting** and growth projections, plus **AI portfolio insights**.
- **Weekly portfolio emails** with market context.
- **Free calculators** that need no login: LTV, liquidation, margin call, DCA, re-borrow, retirement and more.

BitStack is a tracking and planning tool. It is not a lender and doesn't originate loans.

## How it’s built

- **React Native with Expo**, shipping to the web and mobile from one codebase.
- **Firebase** for auth, data and background functions; **Stripe** for free and Pro subscriptions.
- **Gemini** powers the AI portfolio advisor.
- Built since January 2026, almost entirely with **AI coding agents**: I own product, design, review and growth.

## The growth system

BitStack is built to be found, by search engines and by AI assistants. The free calculators each have a no-JavaScript, citation-friendly version, there's a neutral directory of US Bitcoin products, and the site publishes `llms.txt` and agent-readable tool endpoints. It's the same instrument-first, content-led demand engine I build for B2B SaaS, run on a product of my own.
