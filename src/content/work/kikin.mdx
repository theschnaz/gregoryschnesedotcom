## Context

kikin was a New York contextual-search startup: a desktop browser plugin, and later kikin Browser for iPad, that let people look things up without leaving the page they were reading. I joined as a Product Manager in July 2009 and owned user acquisition and growth marketing.

## The problem

Browser-plugin growth is expensive and leaky: installs are easy to buy, active users are not. The job was to grow monthly active users *profitably*, which meant knowing which installs turned into retained users and what they were worth.

## What I did

- **LTV and cohort analysis.** Ran retention experiments and cohort analysis to see which channels and onboarding paths produced users who stuck around, then moved budget accordingly.
- **Built the analytics infrastructure.** Stood up growth tracking and automated reporting dashboards so decisions were made on data rather than install counts.
- **Launched kikin Browser for iPad.** Ran a targeted pre-launch campaign with SEO-optimized landing pages and narrative-driven messaging that produced 5K+ email signups before launch.

## Results

- Grew the user base from roughly **300K to 2M+ monthly active users**, profitably.
- kikin Browser for iPad reached **top-25 and top-10 App Store rankings**, with **50K+ installs on a $28K budget**.

## What I learned

This is where I learned that growth moves fastest when you build the thing yourself. Today that means building in React and React Native, and it's been my operating model ever since.
