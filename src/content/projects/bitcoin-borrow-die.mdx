## What it is

**Bitcoin, Borrow, Die** applies the classic "buy, borrow, die" wealth strategy to Bitcoin: hold the asset, borrow against it to fund living expenses instead of selling, and let the position compound. The site pairs an interactive **modeling tool** with a **weekly newsletter**.

## Why I built it

Most content about borrowing against Bitcoin is either hype or hand-waving. The interesting questions (what loan-to-value is survivable, how drawdowns interact with interest, how long the strategy holds under different return assumptions) are modeling questions. So I built the model.

## How it’s built

- Built with **Replit** and AI agents, from first prototype to production, starting in December 2024.
- Simulates multi-decade scenarios with Bitcoin as collateral, including drawdowns and loan-to-value limits.
- The newsletter is the distribution engine: every issue drives back to the tool.
